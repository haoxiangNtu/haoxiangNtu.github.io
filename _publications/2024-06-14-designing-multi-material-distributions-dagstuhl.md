This contribution describes a complete and efficient pipeline for designing multi-material distributions in 3D parts so that the printed part exhibits a desired deformation behaviour. It covers deformation target specification, material parameterization with differentiable interpolation relaxation, and large-scale discrete optimization, and has been validated through simulation and physical 3D-printed customized insoles.
